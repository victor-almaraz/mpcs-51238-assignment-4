# ADR-0005: Art as One Asset Kind

**Status:** Accepted
**Date:** 2026-10-23
**Deciders:** Project team

## Context

Content icons cover every entity, but authors want portraits for characters and creatures and tiles for terrain, including images made with generative tools. Players want portraits of their characters. Each image needs alt text, a licence, an attribution where required, and a single path for storage, moderation, export, and deletion.

## Decision

Declare every image as an asset in a pack's `assets.yaml`, with `kind` (`icon`, `portrait`, or `tile`), `source`, `alt`, and optional `license`, `credit`, and `provenance`. Content carries one field, `art`, a list of asset refs with at most one per kind. Store files by content hash in one Asset record, shared by pack files and player uploads. Clients choose a kind per context and fall back to icons, initials, shapes, and hatching. Models never receive art.

## Options Considered

| Option | Assessment |
|---|---|
| Separate `icon`, `portrait`, and `tile` fields on each content kind | Rejected. Three fields on every schema, three validation paths, and no shared record for uploads. |
| One `art` list of refs to declared assets (chosen) | One schema change per content kind; refs reuse art across packs; one record and one moderation path. |

## Consequences

- Packs gain `assets.yaml` and `assets/`; the Content Compiler checks refs, files, types, sizes, alt text, and credits.
- Player portraits use the same record, upload limits, sanitization, export, deletion, and content-reports process.
- Changes to the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy for uploads are proposed to the project owner separately.
