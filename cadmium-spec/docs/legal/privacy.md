# Privacy Policy

**Cadmium**
Effective date: [DATE]
Operated by: the Cadmium project, a student project for MPCS 51238 Design, Build, Ship at the University of Chicago
Contact: [EMAIL]

This Privacy Policy describes what personal data Cadmium collects, the purposes for which it is used, the service providers that process it, how long it is retained, and how you can access, export, correct, and delete it. "We" means the operators of Cadmium.

## 1. Summary

- We collect only the data needed to authenticate you and run your games.
- We use no tracking pixels and no analytics or advertising cookies.
- We disclose data only to the service providers that host Cadmium, provide authentication, store data, and run the AI narrator. We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or use it to train AI models.
- Cadmium sends no email. Clerk, our authentication provider, sends sign-in codes and links.
- You can access, export, correct, and delete your data yourself in Settings at any time.

## 2. Data we collect

| Data | Source | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Email address, display name, authentication data | You, through Clerk | Authentication; displaying your name to the players in your campaigns |
| Time zone, theme, reminder, and calendar feed settings | You, in Settings | Displaying session times and sending the reminders you configure |
| Push notification subscriptions | Your browser, when you enable browser notifications | Sending session reminders to that browser |
| Characters, adventures, campaigns, schedules, RSVPs | You | Providing the features you use |
| Character portraits you upload, with their alt text and any license, credit, or provenance note | You | Displaying your character to the players in your campaigns |
| Invitations you create, with their message and note | You | Allowing the recipient of the code to join your campaign |
| Game inputs and the resulting game history | You and the other players in your games | Running games, narration, and game history |
| Technical log data: IP address, browser type, time, and page requested | Your browser, logged by our hosting and database providers | Serving pages, security, and troubleshooting |

A portrait that depicts a real person is that person's personal data. Upload one only with their consent, as the Terms of Service require.

We do not collect payment information, precise location (beyond what an IP address indicates), contacts, or data from other applications. Invitation codes are shared by you, so we never receive the contact details of the people you invite.

The AI narrator interprets game inputs and describes events in the game. It makes no automated decisions about you.

### Visibility to other players

Members of a campaign you belong to can see your display name, your characters, your RSVPs, and all of your actions in its games. No one else can see you: Cadmium has no profiles, user directory, or public listings, and adventures you publish can be used only in campaigns you create.

You may email [EMAIL] during that period to request a copy of your data. After 90 days, all stored data is deleted, except data preserved under section 7.

| Withdrawing reminders | **Settings, Reminders** to disable notifications or remove a browser; **Settings, Calendar** to disable your calendar feed. |

### Deleting your data

Account deletion proceeds in three stages:

1. **Recovery period, days 1 to 30.** Your account is closed and hidden immediately. Signing in during this period allows you to restore it.
2. **Retention period, days 31 to 90.** Your account can no longer be restored and remains hidden from all users. It is retained so that we can handle a report, a dispute, or a request from an authority concerning it, and the operators access it only for those purposes.
3. **Erasure, after day 90.** Your data is permanently deleted.

Erasure removes your account, characters, uploaded images, drafts and published adventures, the campaigns you authored with their games, your push notification subscriptions, and your game inputs in other users' games. Where your name remains in other users' game records, it is replaced with "Former player". Copies in our providers' backups and logs are deleted according to their retention schedules, which may take additional time.

Effects on other users:

- Games in campaigns you authored are deleted.
- Games in your campaigns that use an adventure you published end, and the adventure is removed.
- AI narration in other users' games is stored as free text and may continue to mention your character's name.

Characters, drafts, and campaigns deleted individually follow the same stages. Until they are erased, 90 days after the deletion, they are kept and hidden, and they can be restored for 30 days. Deleting a campaign ends its games, and its members lose access to it at once; their characters stay with them. Archiving a campaign deletes nothing: the campaign and its games stay visible to its members, and its author can unarchive it. Archiving a character or an adventure draft deletes nothing either: an archived character cannot join games, and an archived draft cannot be published, until its owner unarchives it.

Data preserved under section 7 is retained until the matter for which it was preserved is closed, even if you delete it.

### Other requests

To object to or restrict any processing of your data, or for any request not covered by Settings, email [EMAIL] from the address associated with your account. We respond within 30 days, free of charge. If we decline a request, we will explain why, and you may ask us to reconsider. You may also lodge a complaint with a data protection or consumer protection authority.

## 7. Legal disclosures and preservation

We may disclose or retain data beyond what this policy describes where required by law, such as in response to a valid court order, or where necessary to protect the safety of any person or the security of the service.

If content, including an image, appears to be unlawful, we may preserve it together with the related account and game records, including after deletion, and report it to law enforcement or other authorities and to the University of Chicago. Preserved data is retained only until the matter is closed and is then deleted.

Where permitted by law, we will notify you when we disclose or preserve your data under this section.

## 8. Security

Data is encrypted in transit and stored with our service providers, which maintain their own security programs. Access to Cadmium's administrative tools is limited to its operators, who access game content and history only to handle a report, resolve a technical fault, or respond to a request from you. No system is completely secure. If a data breach affects your personal data, we will notify you without undue delay, describing what happened, what data was involved, and what steps you can take.

## 9. Children

Cadmium is not intended for anyone under 16. If we learn that a person under 16 has an account, we will delete it.

## 10. Changes to this policy

We may amend this policy. We will publish the amended version with a new effective date and, for material changes, display a notice when you next sign in.
